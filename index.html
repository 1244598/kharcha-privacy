Kharcha — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

The short version: Kharcha does not collect, transmit or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and does not request internet access.

Kharcha ("the app") is a personal expense tracker made by Yash Laxkar, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores, and where

When you use Kharcha it saves, on your device only:

  • the transactions you enter (amount, expense or income, category, optional note, date and time);
  • the list of categories;
  • your monthly budget, if you set one.

This data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The developer has no access to it.

Data we collect

None. Kharcha does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it does not have permission to access the internet.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, because we never receive it.

Android backup

Kharcha allows Android's built-in backup. If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include Kharcha's data in your device backup, which is stored in your own Google account under Google's terms. This lets you keep your records when you move to a new phone. The developer cannot access these backups. You can turn device backup off in your phone's settings at any time.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual transactions in the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Kharcha → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app.

Children

Kharcha is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.
